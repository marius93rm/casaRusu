# Casa Rusu brand proposals

These are three original logo directions for Restaurant Casa Rusu. They are design proposals pending restaurant approval, not an approved brand manual.

## Concepts

- `concept-1-doorway.svg` / `.png` — a horizontal doorway and threshold mark. The simple CR monogram gives the site a distinctive entrance cue while the wordmark stays readable at header size. This is the provisional site integration.
- `concept-2-roundel.svg` / `.png` — a compact navy roundel with a bold CR and a horizontal wordmark. The square `concept-2-roundel-icon.svg` / `.png` export is intended for social avatars and other square applications; use the horizontal lockup for signage and wider layouts.
- `concept-3-wordmark.svg` / `.png` — a typographic lockup with a minimal plate/table-line detail. It keeps the identity quiet and editorial, with no decorative flourishes.

## Palette and type

- Deep ink/navy: `#122336`
- Warm ivory: `#f8f3e8`
- Muted brass: `#b29862`
- The SVGs use editable text set to Cormorant Garamond and DM Sans with system fallbacks. External SVG renderers may not inherit the site’s locally hosted fonts, so exact glyph shapes can vary; the PNG exports preserve a fixed rendering.
- Artwork has no texture, gradients, shadows, fake depth, or stock icons. The three concept logo SVGs keep wordmark text editable; the standalone favicon draws its CR mark with vector paths.

The PNG exports are transparent RGBA files. The original logo is retained untouched at `public/brand/casa-rusu-logo.jpg` for rollback. On 2026-10-12, the [official Facebook profile](https://www.facebook.com/people/Restaurant-Casa-Rusu/61594316175166/) and [Instagram profile](https://www.instagram.com/restaurant.casarusu/) both showed the same existing logo; no alternate official asset was found. The local exterior sign informed the restrained navy/brass cue only; it is not treated as an approved brand manual.
